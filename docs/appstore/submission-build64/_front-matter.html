1. App Review Reply — Guideline 1.4.1 & 2.1

Hello, and thank you for the continued review. Below we address Guideline 1.4.1 with formal accuracy documentation, and Guideline 2.1 with the requested face-data details.

Guideline 1.4.1 — Safety (Physical Harm): accuracy data & methodology

The health-related indicators in MetaHint are not produced by the bare device sensors. They are produced by a certified third-party SDK, FzljtiliSignal (Binah.ai), version 5.11.4 (the exact version bundled in this build), which uses remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) — it analyzes subtle color changes in the facial skin captured by the camera. This is an established, peer-validated measurement method, not an unsupported claim about raw sensors.

Positioning. MetaHint is a general fitness and wellness app, not a medical or diagnostic product. A disclaimer on the results screen and in the App Store description states the values are "not intended for medical use, including self-diagnosis or consultation with a doctor, and are for general fitness and wellness only."

Sensor-only prohibition is respected. On iOS, all clinically sensitive outputs — blood pressure, blood oxygen (SpO2), and every disease-risk index (e.g., ASCVD, HbA1c, cholesterol, glucose, hemoglobin) — are not displayed, stored, or transmitted. The app therefore makes no sensor-only blood-pressure / blood-oxygen claim. The indicators shown on iOS are limited to pulse rate, respiration rate, heart-rate variability (HRV), and a stress index (the stress index is derived from HRV; it is not a separate sensor reading).

Accuracy documentation. We attach (a) the SDK vendor's official accuracy reports for the exact version in our app, and (b) independent peer-reviewed clinical validations of the same rPPG method. Each report discloses the reference device, sample size, and quantitative error.

Guideline 2.1 — Information about face data

  1. What face data is collected. For the wellness scan, a live facial video stream is processed in real time on-device by the Binah SDK to derive rPPG signals; the raw video is not retained. For skin analysis, a single facial photo is captured.
  2. All uses. Facial video → on-device computation of wellness indicators (pulse, respiration, HRV, stress). Skin photo → skin-condition analysis. No advertising, no profiling, no facial recognition/identification.
  3. Third parties & storage. The wellness facial video never leaves the device (processed on-device by the SDK); only the resulting numeric values are saved to our backend. The skin photo is sent to YouCam (PerfectCorp) for analysis; we store only the resulting scores.
  4. Retention. Raw facial video: not stored (discarded immediately after real-time processing). Skin photo: handled per PerfectCorp policy, not retained long-term by us. Numeric results: kept until the user deletes them; users can delete individual records in-app, and all data is deleted on account deletion.
  5. Privacy policy location. Disclosed in the "Collection & Use of Personal Information" and "Provision & Consignment to Third Parties" sections, which include a dedicated face/biometric data clause. Accessible in-app via Profile → Terms / Privacy Policy and at our public privacy URL.
  6. Exact privacy-policy text (face data): "For wellness measurement, the facial video is processed in real time on the device by FzljtiliSignal (Binah); the original video is not stored or transmitted — only numeric values are saved to our server. For skin analysis, the facial photo is provided to a third party (YouCam, PerfectCorp) located overseas, and we store only the resulting values. Users can delete individual records in the app, and all related data is destroyed upon account withdrawal. Before any data is transmitted, the app explains what is sent, to whom, and why, and obtains consent, which can be withdrawn at any time in Data Consent Settings."

Consent is off by default for new users, persistent, shown before any data leaves the device, and withdrawable anytime in Profile → Data Consent Settings (re-consent required on next use).

We are happy to provide any further documentation. Thank you.
